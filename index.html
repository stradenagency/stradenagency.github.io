Where growth gets expensive

The work is there. The gaps are costing you.

Revenue and capacity disappear when work stalls between people, tools, and next steps. The business stays busy, but too much effort goes into holding the operation together instead of completing valuable work.

Revenue leaks between the steps

Leads wait for follow-up. Estimates stall. Completed jobs do not trigger invoices. Payment issues surface later than they should.

Capacity disappears into coordination

Skilled people spend their time searching, copying, checking status, re-entering information, and reminding someone else what happens next.

Growth creates more owner dependency

Exceptions, approvals, and context keep routing through the same person, so more work creates more chasing instead of more operating leverage.

The cost shows up twice: revenue that arrives late or never arrives, and hours your team cannot spend producing, serving customers, or growing the business.

Something happens. The right agent reads the context, uses the skills for its job, moves the workflow across your tools, produces the next output, and brings judgment back to your team when approval matters.

Straight answers

Questions people ask us.

What does Straden actually build?+

Straden builds and operates intelligent AI agents that perform defined jobs across your business. Each agent receives the skills, instructions, tool access, memory, and decision boundaries required for its role. Related agents can form systems for revenue, operations, customer service, finance, reporting, and other functions. We connect those systems over time to create the operating system your team works through.

Where do we start?+

We start with the workflow — or closely connected group of workflows — creating the greatest loss of time, revenue, or operating capacity. That could be one problem, such as lead follow-up, or a connected set such as scheduling, completed-job handoffs, invoicing, and payment tracking. We identify the smallest meaningful system that can solve the underlying problem, prove it, and then expand into adjacent workflows.

Will we have to replace the tools we already use?+

Usually, no. Straden is designed to coordinate work across the tools your business already depends on, including email, file storage, accounting platforms, customer records, spreadsheets, project systems, and other connected applications. If an existing tool creates a serious limitation, we explain that during the Full Audit rather than quietly forcing a replacement.

How do the agents work with our team?+

Agents monitor connected workflows, interpret relevant context, use their assigned skills, and move work toward its next required outcome. Depending on the role, an agent might route a request, prepare an estimate, update a project record, organize a document, trigger an invoice, draft a customer response, or flag an exception. Important decisions and sensitive actions can be configured to require approval from the appropriate person.

What happens if an agent gets something wrong?+

We design approval boundaries, validation steps, exception handling, and escalation paths around the risk of each workflow. Low-risk, repeatable work may proceed automatically. Actions involving customers, money, sensitive information, or significant business judgment can require review before completion. When something is unclear, the system should pause, surface the relevant context, and ask the appropriate person instead of inventing an answer.

How do the agents improve over time?+

Agents can retain approved business context, decisions, outcomes, corrections, and operating knowledge so they become more useful within your organization. That does not mean uncontrolled self-learning. Memory, feedback, permissions, and behaviour are governed within the system so agents can understand your business more accurately while remaining inside their defined roles and approval boundaries.

What happens during the Full Audit, and how long does implementation take?+

The Full Audit begins with a focused 45-to-60-minute working session covering the main pain point, connected workflows, tools, information, handoffs, approvals, and business impact. Following the session, Straden delivers the diagnosis, recommended system or systems, proposed workflows and integrations, approval boundaries, implementation plan, timeline, and cost. Implementation timing depends on that scope and moves through design, build, testing, launch, and ongoing operation.

What does this cost?+

The Full Audit is $1,000 CAD. If you proceed with the recommended system, the full audit fee is credited toward implementation. Implementation pricing depends on the workflows, integrations, data, approvals, and systems involved. Your plan will include the implementation cost and the monthly retainer required for Straden to operate, monitor, maintain, and improve the system.

Who owns our data and business information?+

You retain ownership of your business data, records, documents, and intellectual property. Straden retains its reusable software frameworks, general methods, agent skills, templates, and other underlying components. Permitted cross-business analysis uses anonymized information designed not to identify the originating client, and clients may opt out. More detail is available in our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
