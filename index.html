One agent team, built around the way your business already works.

Agents do the work, not just coordinate it. They hold the context, carry out business functions, keep work moving, follow through, and bring decisions to the right person. That can mean running one function deeply or connecting steps across several functions to solve a wider operational problem.

  1. Sales systemCapture and qualify leads, nurture opportunities, prepare estimates or proposals, and keep follow-up moving.
  2. Project operations systemTurn approved work into active jobs, create the project structure, track milestones, and route approvals or exceptions.
  3. Finance systemTrigger invoices, track payment status, follow up on outstanding balances, and keep revenue visible.
  4. Lead-to-payment systemCapture the lead, prepare the estimate or proposal, track the work through completion, trigger invoicing, and follow through until payment.

We start with the function or workflow costing the most time or revenue. Once it works, we add the adjacent agents until the operation runs front to back.

Straight answers before you start.

What does Straden actually build?+

Straden builds and operates AI agents for specific business workflows, from lead intake and estimates to delivery, invoicing, reporting, and follow-up. We start with one workflow and expand only when the next connection is useful.

Do we need to replace our current tools?+

Usually, no. We design the agents to work across the tools your business already depends on. If a tool creates a serious limitation, we flag it during the Leverage Audit.

What can the agents do without approval?+

That depends on the workflow's risk. Low-risk, repeatable steps may run automatically. Actions that affect customers, money, or business judgment can pause for the appropriate person.

How long does implementation take?+

Timing depends on the workflows, integrations, information, and approval boundaries involved. The Leverage Audit defines the first system and the implementation plan before we commit to a timeline.

What does it cost?+

Implementation and ongoing operating costs depend on the system the audit recommends. Your plan sets out the scope, timeline, implementation cost, and ongoing retainer before you decide whether to proceed.

Who owns our data and operating knowledge?+

You retain ownership of your business data, records, documents, and intellectual property. Straden retains its reusable frameworks and underlying components. More detail is available in our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
