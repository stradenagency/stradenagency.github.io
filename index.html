    Good questions

    A few things you
    might want to know.

    Is this AI?+

    Yes. AI works as a teammate alongside your people, not in place of them.

    Is it safe?+

    Important money and client actions wait for a person's approval, and your data stays yours.

    Is this software my team has to learn?+

    No. Straden builds and operates the system around your work. Your people reach the agents the way they already work, for example by email. Your own setup is planned during the audit.

    Do I need to know which agents I need?+

    No. Start with the work that causes the most friction. We map the right agent roles and expand from there.

    Will agents take important actions on their own?+

    Important money and client-facing actions have a human approval point. Your team keeps the judgment.

    Does this only work for a certain industry?+

    We work with service businesses that have people, tools, and handoffs to coordinate. The first agent team is shaped around your actual bottleneck.

    What happens after the build?+

    Straden operates, monitors, and improves the system as the work changes. The service continues beyond installation.

    Who is this not for?+

    A business that does not want AI in it. Straden builds AI that works alongside your team, and if that is not what you want, we are not the right fit.
