Privacy notice

20 October 2026 · Product waitlist

This notice covers Straden’s service-trades and project-delivery preview pages. It does not describe a deployed client agent or replace a client’s service agreement.

Who is responsible

Straden is operated by Jamal Muckett-Sobers, a sole proprietor in Ontario, Canada. For privacy questions, access or correction requests, deletion requests, or complaints, contact hello@straden.ca.

What this preview collects

The waitlist form collects your name, business email and annual revenue range in CAD or USD, plus a phone number if you choose to provide one, plus your consent to product and early-access emails. We save the service or project page you joined from and any campaign labels in its link. Typing an email into the page alone does not submit it; we save your details only when you complete the popup and submit it. The early-access form on the straden.ca home page asks for the same details plus your type of business, which tells us whether you run a service or a project business; if you choose Other, we keep the description you type. If you tick the early-access box at the end of the free Mini Audit, we add the first name, email, phone number and type of business you gave in the audit to the same waitlist; the audit does not ask for revenue. Completing the audit without ticking that box does not add you to the waitlist. The illustrated conversations, documents and work records are examples; they do not access your company’s systems or submit information to an agent.

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any details you choose to include. Please do not send confidential client records, credentials or sensitive project information.

How we use information

We use correspondence to answer your questions and discuss the product or early access when you request it. Contacting us is not automatic consent to unrelated marketing. We do not sell your contact details.

Hosting and measurement

These waitlist pages are hosted through GitHub Pages, with form processing through OpenAI Sites. The hosting provider may process sign-in information and technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to deliver and secure the site, under its own privacy policy. Google Analytics and Meta Pixel load only if you accept optional measurement. They measure page visits, completed waitlist signups and which pages or ads brought them. We do not send your name, email, phone or annual revenue to these tools. Google and Meta may use browser/cookie identifiers and technical information under their own privacy policies. Declining does not prevent joining the waitlist. Use Privacy choices in the footer to decline or change your choice; this stops future measurement on these pages. Campaign labels and the landing page are saved with a completed signup even if you decline optional measurement, to help us understand interest. Email-update consent is separate from measurement consent. Fonts are served with the site.

Providers, retention and your choices

Our hosting and email providers process information needed to provide their services; processing may occur outside Canada. We keep correspondence while needed to handle your enquiry and related business or legal obligations, then delete it when no longer needed. You can ask us to stop contacting you, or request access, correction or deletion, using the email above. We will explain any limits that apply. No online service can guarantee complete security.

Waitlist storage and choices

Completed signups are stored in Straden’s Supabase database. Our website server submits the form; database credentials stay on the server. We use them to understand demand, contact you about the product and send the updates you requested. Revenue is used to understand business size and fit. Signup entries older than 24 months are removed when the form next processes a submission. You may request earlier removal or withdraw email consent at hello@straden.ca. No marketing emails or SMS are automatically sent by this form.
