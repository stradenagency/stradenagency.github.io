Why Straden exists

Most teams end up holding it all together by hand.

Jobs, clients and conversations pile up, and the work only moves because a few key people remember it, chase it and carry it in their heads. It works, until the business grows past what those people can hold.

AI changes that. Agents can now take on real work: drafting estimates and proposals, following up, keeping projects on track, preparing invoices. Straden builds those agents around how a business already runs, connects them to the tools it already uses, and runs them so the team doesn't have to.

You get the leverage and productivity from AI. We handle the rest.

What we believe

Four ideas behind every system we build.

  1. 01AI belongs on the team, not in place of it.Agents take on the work so your people can focus on what needs them.
  2. 02Your business should keep moving without everything depending on you.The work carries forward even when key people are busy or away.
  3. 03People keep the judgment; the system carries the work forward.Money and client actions wait for your approval.
  4. 04Start where it hurts most, then grow.One agent on your biggest bottleneck first, then the rest when you're ready.
